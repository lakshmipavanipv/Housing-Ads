About this Home
Why this flat stands apart

Perched on the 9th floor of B Block in the award-winning Sukhii Ubuntu community, this 1,315 sq. ft. west-facing corner flat is an exceptionally rare find. Every Ubuntu home is a corner flat — yours opens to warm golden afternoon light and lush views of the 400+ acre Khajaguda Hills.

The layout features a generous Master Bedroom (10′4″ × 15′), spacious Drawing Room, open Dining area, a well-designed kitchen with utility, a private Sit Out and landscaped Balcony — all cross-ventilated for maximum airflow. The 9th floor is the sweet spot: cool breeze, no road noise, open views without obstruction.

Ready to move. 1 covered parking included. Smart home automation, wireless metering for gas/electricity/water, vitrified flooring. RERA approved — safe, transparent transaction. Located just 0.5 km from ORR and 2 minutes from the Financial District.
